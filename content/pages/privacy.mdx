# Privacy Policy

Wappkit is designed to keep the website model simple and collect only the information needed to run product pages, checkout flows, license retrieval, and support.

## What this policy covers

This policy covers:

- the Wappkit website
- checkout and license retrieval pages
- desktop products that connect to Wappkit license services

## Information we may process

Depending on the page or product flow, Wappkit may process:

- purchase email
- order ID
- license key
- product identifier
- device identifier used for license activation or deactivation
- support emails or messages you send us

We only intend to use this information for product delivery, payment follow-up, license validation, license recovery, support, and abuse prevention.

## License activation and recovery

Some Wappkit desktop products use a license-based activation model instead of a web account dashboard.

For those products, Wappkit may:

- validate a license key
- bind a license to a device
- remove a license binding from a device
- help the customer retrieve a lost key through the license retrieval flow

For example, `Wappkit App Setup` may send license-related data to Wappkit services so the app can validate Premium access and manage device binding.

## Local data on your device

Some desktop products may store a local license session file on the user's device so the app can remember activation state.

For `Wappkit App Setup`, that may include a local file under:

- `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Wappkit\AppSetup\license-session.json`

The website itself does not control the user's local file system, but this local behavior is part of the product experience and is disclosed here for clarity.

## Third-party services

Wappkit may rely on third-party services for:

- payment processing
- transaction records
- email delivery
- hosting and deployment

Those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

## Support requests

If you contact Wappkit for support, we may keep the information needed to answer the request, follow up on a license issue, or resolve billing and activation problems.

## No unnecessary account system

Wappkit products are intentionally designed to avoid a heavy account model when it is not needed.

Where possible, customers can:

- buy through the website
- activate inside the app
- recover the license with order details

This reduces the amount of user account data we need to store.

## Analytics and advertising

As of this policy update, Wappkit does not describe desktop product usage with an in-app advertising SDK or a mandatory account analytics dashboard by default.

If that changes materially, this policy should be updated.

## Contact

For privacy or support questions, contact Wappkit through:

- `/contact`
- or the relevant product support page, such as `/tools/wappkit-app-setup/support`

## Policy updates

Wappkit may update this page from time to time. The current effective date should remain visible on the public website.
